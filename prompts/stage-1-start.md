# Stage 1 — Start

Turn the request in this packet into a clear decision.

## Produce

- The decision to be made
- Who makes it
- The actions that follow from each plausible result
- The limits of the request (time, scope, what is out of bounds)

## Constraints

- Do not look at data values. If any data is in the packet, refuse it and say so.
- Do not invent stakeholders, actions, or limits that the request does not support. If the request is unclear, say what is missing.
- Keep the write-up short and exact.

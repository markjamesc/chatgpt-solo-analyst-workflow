# Stage 2 — Framing

Turn the sealed Stage 1 decision into one precise, locked question.

## Produce

One question, plus:

- The population or target (who or what is measured)
- The time window
- The metric, defined in words
- The threshold that would change the decision
- A capacity stance: what this question can and cannot decide

## Constraints

- Do not look at data values. If any data is in the packet, refuse it and say so.
- Do not add a second question, and do not reshape the question to fit data you have not been shown.
- If the sealed Stage 1 decision cannot support one precise question, say what is missing instead of guessing.
